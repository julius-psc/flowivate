Indeed, efficiency comes when you strategise in the best way to get to your goal but that doesn't necessarily mean working harder. People who grow wealth are the ones that hire people to do the long redundant work for them, they don't do it themselves and the result is that they get larger amounts of work done in a shorter span without working harder. The user does not believe that doing multiple things at the same time will help them be more efficient in the long-term.
